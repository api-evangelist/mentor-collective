Boston, MA. (October 5, 2026) Mentor Collective, higher education's preferred edtech platform for student mentorship, announced the launch of Flash Mentoring, a new, purposeful model built around focused, single-session conversations between students and alumni. Flash Mentoring joins Mentor Collective's existing Structured Mentoring model to support the full arc of the student journey, from belonging to employability, on one platform.
