For VPs, deans, and higher education leaders facing mounting pressure around student persistence and equitable outcomes, implementing a peer mentorship program often feels like a balancing act between resource constraints and impact. While most leaders intuitively recognize mentorship as a high-impact practice, few institutions can reliably isolate its return on investment—let alone scale it across an entire college without exhausting staff capacity.
